Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026 · Applies to Tuckaway 2.0 and later, both the Microsoft Store version and the version downloaded from the website
✓Stays on your PCYour clipboard history is stored only on your computer, encrypted.
✓No trackingNo analytics, no ads, no crash reports, no account.
✓Nothing is sold or sharedWe never see your clips, so there is nothing to share.

What Tuckaway stores

Tuckaway is a clipboard manager. To do its job, it saves what you copy, so you can find and paste it again later. This can include:

  • Text, links, images and files you copy.
  • The name of the program you copied from, and the time.
  • Screenshots you take with Tuckaway, and any marks you add to them.
  • Your settings, folders, tags, favorites, snippets, macros and sticky notes.
  • A local log of the app's activity (see The log).

Your clips are kept in a database file on your PC that is encrypted with a random key made for your PC. That key is protected with Windows data protection for your user account, so the database can only be opened by your Windows account. Stored images and copied files are encrypted too. Snippets, macros and settings are stored without encryption. Nothing is ever uploaded by Tuckaway.

To know which program you copied from, and to notice a screen share (Teams, Zoom and similar) so it can hide your clips, Tuckaway reads the names and titles of open windows. This happens only on your PC, and window titles are not written to the log.

What Tuckaway does not store

  • Anything a program marks as private. Password managers and many other apps tell Windows that a copy should not be recorded, and Tuckaway always honours that, whatever its settings.
  • Copies from programs you add to Ignore programs in Settings, and, if you turn that option on, copies from private browser windows ("Also ignore private windows").
  • Anything copied while Do not disturb is on, or while the app is locked with "Pause capture while locked" turned on in Settings, App Lock.

When Tuckaway detects something that looks like a password, a key or another secret, Secret Guard seals it, and it can delete such clips automatically after a time you choose.

Keyboard and mouse

Tuckaway sees key presses only while it needs them: while its window is in front, so you can move through your clips with the keyboard; if you turn on snippets, so an abbreviation you type can be replaced with its text (only then does it also notice mouse clicks); while you paste a session's clips one by one; and while the first-run "Try your shortcut" bar is showing. Key presses are never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Things that happen only on your PC

  • Search, including search by meaning.
  • Reading text from images (Copy text), using the text recognition built into Windows.
  • Detecting passwords, links, emails and phone numbers in your clips.
  • Windows Hello, when you use it to unlock the app or reveal a secret.

The log

Tuckaway keeps a log file on your PC to help with problems. At the normal log level (Info, the level of every new install and of Restore to defaults) it never contains the content of your clips, file names or folder paths, and your Windows user name and profile folder are removed from every line. If you raise the log level to Debug in Settings, Log, for troubleshooting, lines can also include folder and file paths (such as the names of files you copied) and names you give clips; Tuckaway says so when you turn it on, and goes back to Info by itself after 24 hours. The log is never sent anywhere; Save diagnostics only makes a zip file for you to share if you choose.

Your control

  • Delete clips one by one, in bulk, or automatically by age or number (Settings, History). Automatic clearing moves clips to Recovery Trash first, as far as the Trash has room.
  • Clear all history from Settings, Storage.
  • Erase everything from Settings, General, Restore to defaults, with "Also erase all my data".
  • Lock the app with App Lock, so others using your PC can't open your history. Your PIN is stored only as a hash.
  • Uninstall Tuckaway to remove the app. Uninstalling the Microsoft Store version also removes all of its data. Uninstalling the version downloaded from the website keeps your clips and settings in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Tuckaway until you delete that folder, so a reinstall finds them again.

Sharing and exports

Tuckaway does not share your data with anyone. When you export clips, the file is saved only where you choose. A database export is not encrypted, so anyone who opens the file can read your clips; Tuckaway warns you before it writes one. In a clips export (.tuckaway), clips locked with a password stay locked, and Secret Guard clips are left out unless you include them with a password of your choice.

Children

Tuckaway is not directed at children and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If a change affects what leaves your PC, it will also be mentioned in the release notes.
