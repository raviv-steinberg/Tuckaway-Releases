Everything you copy, ready when you need it

Tuckaway is a clipboard manager for Windows that keeps everything you copy in a searchable history on your PC, encrypted, so you can find it and paste it again with one shortcut.

Coming soon to Microsoft Store See the screenshots

What it does

Find anything you copiedType to search your whole history, with the words highlighted. Filter by kind, program, folder or tag.
Search by meaningOptional: "bill" also finds an invoice. It runs entirely on your PC.
Paste fasterEnter or a number key pastes into the program you were in, with or without formatting.
Paste queue and Quick SlotsCollect clips and paste them in order, or keep nine on their own shortcuts.
OrganizeFavorites, folders, smart folders that fill themselves, tags and workspaces.
Capture SessionsCopies made together are grouped, so you can paste them all at once.
Secret Guard and App LockPasswords and keys are sealed as you copy them; a PIN locks the history.
Private by designYour clips stay on your PC, encrypted. No account, no tracking, no ads.
